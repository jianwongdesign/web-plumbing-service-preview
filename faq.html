8 questions

What should I include in an enquiry?
Tell us what is happening, where the problem is, your property type and your six-digit postal code. A clear description of when the problem started is helpful. For a real booking, a photo or short video can also help the plumber assess the next step.
Do I need a PUB Licensed Plumber?
Regulated water service and sanitary plumbing works in Singapore must be carried out by a PUB Licensed Plumber. Some simple plumbing tasks are exempt. Check the scope and the individual plumber’s licence before arranging work. Read PUB’s plumbing guidance (opens in a new tab).
How much will the work cost?
The cost depends on the problem, access, parts and work involved. Ask for a clear quotation and confirmation of any assessment or call-out charge before proceeding. Prices are not included in this mockup because the company has not supplied its rates.
Can I arrange an urgent visit?
Urgent appointments, operating hours and response times need to be confirmed directly with the company. This demonstration does not accept bookings or provide emergency support.
Can I enquire if I am renting the property?
Yes, you can prepare an enquiry. Check with your landlord or managing agent before authorising work, and confirm who will arrange access and approve the quotation.
What if I am not sure which service I need?
Choose “Something else / not sure” in the enquiry form and describe what you can see or hear. You do not need to diagnose the problem yourself.
Which parts of Singapore are covered?
The mockup presents a Singapore-wide service concept, including HDB flats, condominiums and landed homes. Actual coverage, access arrangements and appointment availability must be confirmed with the company.
Does the enquiry form send a real message?
No. This is a design preview. The form creates an enquiry summary inside your browser so you can try the experience. Nothing is sent, booked or saved to a server. Please use sample details.
